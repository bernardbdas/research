# Mandatory Scholarly Citation Rule

Whenever explaining concepts, comparing algorithms, asserting performance claims, or
deriving mathematical formulations:

1. **Always cite formal literature**: Mention Author(s), Year, Title, and Venue/arXiv.
1. **Key Foundations to Reference**:
   - **FedAvg**: McMahan et al. (2017), *Communication-Efficient Learning of Deep
     Networks from Decentralized Data*, AISTATS.
   - **FedProx & Heterogeneity**: Li et al. (2020), *Federated Optimization in
     Heterogeneous Networks*, MLSys.
   - **Client Drift & SCAFFOLD**: Karimireddy et al. (2020), *SCAFFOLD: Stochastic
     Controlled Averaging for Federated Learning*, ICML.
   - **Robust Aggregation**: Yin et al. (2018), *Byzantine-Robust Distributed Learning:
     Towards Optimal Statistical Rates*, ICML.
   - **Differential Privacy**: Dwork et al. (2006), *Calibrating Noise to Sensitivity in
     Private Data Analysis*, TCC; Abadi et al. (2016), *Deep Learning with Differential
     Privacy*, ACM CCS.
   - **Homomorphic Encryption**: Paillier (1999), *Public-Key Cryptosystems Based on
     Composite Degree Residuosity Classes*, EUROCRYPT.
   - **Secure Multi-Party Computation**: Shamir (1979), *How to Share a Secret*, CACM;
     Bonawitz et al. (2017), *Practical Secure Aggregation for Privacy-Preserving
     Machine Learning*, ACM CCS.
   - **Adaptive Federated Optimization (FedAdam/FedYogi)**: Reddi et al. (2020),
     *Adaptive Federated Optimization*, ICLR.
   - **Matrix Polar Orthogonalization (FedMuon)**: Takezawa et al. (2025), *FedMuon:
     Federated Learning with Bias-Corrected Matrix Polar Orthogonalization*, arXiv.
   - **Test-Time Training (TTT)**: Sun et al. (2020), *Test-Time Training with
     Self-Supervision for Generalization under Distribution Shifts*, ICML; Sun et al.
     (2024), *Learning to (Learn at Test Time): RNNs with Expressive Hidden States*,
     arXiv.
   - **Titans & Neural Long-Term Memory**: Behrouz et al. (2024), *Titans: Learning to
     Memorize at Test Time*, arXiv.
   - **Continual & Class-Incremental Learning**: Kirkpatrick et al. (2017), *Overcoming
     Catastrophic Forgetting in Neural Networks*, PNAS.
